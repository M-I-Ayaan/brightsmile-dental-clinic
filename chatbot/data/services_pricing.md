# BrightSmile Dental Clinic — Services & Pricing (₹ INR)

This is the clinic's official price list. All prices are approximate starting
prices per visit and may vary based on case complexity.

## General Checkup & Cleaning
Routine oral exam, scaling, and polishing to remove plaque and tartar buildup.
**Price:** ₹800 – ₹1,500 per visit
**Duration:** 30–45 minutes
**Recommended Specialist:** Dr. Emily Carter (General Dentist)
**Best for:** Anyone due for a 6-month checkup, mild bad breath, or visible tartar.

## Cavity Filling
Removal of decay and filling with tooth-colored composite resin.
**Price:** ₹1,200 – ₹3,000 per tooth
**Duration:** 30–60 minutes
**Recommended Specialist:** Dr. Emily Carter (General Dentist)
**Best for:** Tooth pain when eating sweets, visible dark spots on a tooth.

## Root Canal Therapy
Removes infected pulp from inside the tooth to relieve pain and save the tooth
from extraction.
**Price:** ₹4,000 – ₹9,000 per tooth (excluding crown)
**Duration:** 1–2 sessions, 60–90 minutes each
**Recommended Specialist:** Dr. Michael Chen (Oral Surgeon / Endodontics)
**Best for:** Severe, throbbing tooth pain, sensitivity to hot/cold that lingers,
swelling near a tooth.

## Teeth Whitening
Professional in-office bleaching for a visibly brighter smile in one session.
**Price:** ₹5,000 – ₹8,000 for full mouth
**Duration:** 45–60 minutes
**Recommended Specialist:** Dr. Emily Carter (Cosmetic Dentistry)
**Best for:** Yellowing or stained teeth, before a wedding/event.

## Veneers (Cosmetic Bonding)
Thin porcelain or composite shells bonded to the front of teeth to fix shape,
gaps, or discoloration.
**Price:** ₹8,000 – ₹15,000 per tooth
**Duration:** 2 sessions
**Recommended Specialist:** Dr. Emily Carter (Cosmetic Dentistry)
**Best for:** Chipped teeth, gaps, permanent staining that whitening can't fix.

## Orthodontics — Metal Braces
Traditional braces to gradually straighten misaligned teeth.
**Price:** ₹25,000 – ₹45,000 (full treatment, 18–24 months)
**Recommended Specialist:** Dr. James Whitfield (Orthodontist)
**Best for:** Crooked or crowded teeth, overbite/underbite, mostly teens and adults.

## Orthodontics — Clear Aligners
Removable, near-invisible aligners for mild-to-moderate teeth straightening.
**Price:** ₹60,000 – ₹90,000 (full treatment, 6–12 months)
**Recommended Specialist:** Dr. James Whitfield (Orthodontist)
**Best for:** Adults who want a discreet option and have mild misalignment.

## Dental Implants
A titanium post placed in the jaw topped with a crown, replacing a missing
tooth permanently.
**Price:** ₹25,000 – ₹45,000 per implant (including crown)
**Duration:** 2–3 sessions over 3–6 months
**Recommended Specialist:** Dr. Michael Chen (Oral Surgeon)
**Best for:** One or more missing teeth, loose or failing teeth needing replacement.

## Pediatric Dentistry — Checkup & Fluoride Treatment
Gentle checkup, cleaning, and fluoride application for children's teeth.
**Price:** ₹600 – ₹1,200 per visit
**Duration:** 20–30 minutes
**Recommended Specialist:** Dr. Sofia Martinez (Pediatric Dentist)
**Best for:** Children ages 2–12, first dental visits, cavity prevention.

## Emergency Dental Care
Same-day treatment for severe pain, broken teeth, or dental trauma.
**Price:** ₹1,500 – ₹5,000 (consultation + immediate treatment, varies by issue)
**Duration:** Same-day, walk-in priority
**Recommended Specialist:** Dr. Michael Chen (on-call) or next available dentist
**Best for:** Sudden severe pain, a knocked-out or badly broken tooth, facial swelling.

## Wisdom Tooth Extraction
Surgical or simple removal of impacted or problematic wisdom teeth.
**Price:** ₹3,000 – ₹10,000 per tooth (higher for impacted teeth)
**Duration:** 30–60 minutes
**Recommended Specialist:** Dr. Michael Chen (Oral Surgeon)
**Best for:** Jaw pain at the back of the mouth, swelling near the last molar.
